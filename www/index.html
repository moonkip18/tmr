science notes
  • Each item owns a deterministic cue tone, persisted with the item.
  • Learn items first; TMR only replays what you already encoded.
  • Keep volume low — loud cues wake you and reverse the benefit.
  • Cues play in blocks (10 min on / 8 min off) after a 60-min lead-in.
  • The alignment readout is a simulation from population averages — never a measurement of your sleep. Everything stays on this machine.
